3. Project objective

Develop an intelligent, autonomous construction logistics dispatcher that uses player-authorized ships to supply construction storage throughout the X4 universe.

The player controls fleet eligibility, operating modes, priorities, and restrictions.

GUNPIRE handles:

- Construction demand detection
- Supplier discovery
- Ship matching
- Cargo and ware reservations
- Trade execution
- Delivery scheduling
- Fleet distribution
- Supply shortage management
- Safe temporary ship takeover
- Original behavior restoration
- Failure recovery
- Save/load persistence
- Operational visibility

The system must operate without constant player intervention.

Core objective: minimal micromanagement, maximum autonomy, dependable behavior.

4. Required operational modes

Active — Construction Surge

Temporarily borrow eligible player-selected ships from existing automation.

Supported origins should include, where safely verified:

- Galaxy Trader MK3
- Vanilla automatic traders
- Station-assigned traders
- Fleet/subordinate traders

Requirements:

- Never interrupt a ship with active trade cargo or outstanding commitments.
- Wait until the ship is genuinely eligible.
- Snapshot its existing behavior and assignment before takeover.
- Transfer control to GUNPIRE only after snapshot validation.
- Complete bounded construction deliveries.
- Reevaluate requirements after every completed job.
- Restore the original automation when released.
- Preserve pilot progression, trader settings, fleet membership, and assignment.

If an origin cannot be restored reliably, exclude that ship type or assignment from automatic takeover until supported.

Passive — Autonomous Construction Supply

Use dedicated GUNPIRE ships to continuously satisfy construction shortages.

Requirements:

- Discover unsatisfied construction demand.
- Prioritize actual need rather than trade profitability.
- Reevaluate sites dynamically.
- Increase priority for sites waiting unusually long.
- Avoid starvation of low-priority sites.
- Remain autonomous across repeated deliveries.
- Respect player restrictions.

Intelligent Surge

Implement the approved construction urgency and ship-class distribution system.

- Prioritize materials required by the next construction module where safely detectable.
- Match S/M ships to small urgent deliveries.
- Match L/XL ships to substantial bulk shortages.
- Avoid wasting large freighters on trivial quantities.
- Respect cargo capacity and operating geography.
- Keep the fleet dynamically allocated rather than permanently assigning every ship to one site.

If construction queue inspection is not exposed through a verified API, implement the safest documented fallback and clearly identify the limitation.

5. Critical ship safety contract

This section overrides convenience and optimization.

Before taking control of any ship:

1. Confirm explicit enrollment.
2. Confirm the ship remains player-owned and valid.
3. Confirm it has no active trade cargo.
4. Confirm no unresolved trade, transfer, or reservation commitments.
5. Capture its original default behavior and parameters.
6. Capture relevant commander, subordinate, home, and range relationships.
7. Capture the required settings for restoring its prior automation.
8. Persist a restoration record.
9. Validate that the restoration information is sufficient.
10. Only then initiate takeover.

Never blindly clear orders.

Never directly overwrite Galaxy Trader progression tables to simulate restoration.

On release:

- Allow an already loaded GUNPIRE delivery to finish safely.
- Prevent new allocations.
- Release GUNPIRE reservations.
- Restore the original automation and assignment.
- Verify restored state.
- Preserve recovery data when verification fails.

Implement a visible "RESTORE_REQUIRED" condition instead of silently issuing a generic idle order when restoration cannot be established.

Account for manual player intervention, destroyed ships, missing stations, changed commanders, failed purchases, and save/reload.

6. Dynamic logistics scheduler

Build a shared scheduler serving all active construction sites.

Required capabilities:

- Detect construction storage and outstanding ware demand.
- Read on-hand quantities.
- Account for inbound commitments.
- Maintain a separate GUNPIRE reservation ledger.
- Prevent double allocation.
- Discover valid suppliers.
- Verify supplier stock and prices.
- Respect source restrictions.
- Match ships by cargo capacity and distance.
- Prioritize site urgency, shortage severity, and waiting time.
- Prevent permanent site starvation.
- Recover from failed or invalidated routes.
- Reevaluate after delivery.

Supported destination selection:

- All construction storages
- Targeted construction storage
- Closest eligible construction storage

Supported sourcing:

- Player-owned suppliers only
- Non-player suppliers only
- Both

Supported optimization preferences:

- Distance versus purchase cost
- Closest supplier to ship
- Closest supplier to destination
- Relevant construction urgency

One committed bounded job per ship at a time.

Do not create endless static trade-order queues.

Multi-stop optimization is secondary to proving single-delivery reliability.

7. Reservation integrity

Implement persistent reservation tracking for:

- Ship
- Supplier
- Destination
- Ware
- Quantity
- Job
- Reservation state

Prevent duplicated commitments, including those caused by:

- Concurrent scheduling
- Repeated dispatcher execution
- Save/load

- Supplier stock changes
- Ship destruction
- Destination completion
- Manual intervention

Do not assume externally reported incoming quantities are independent of GUNPIRE reservations.

Verify their semantics before calculating net shortages.

Use idempotent state transitions and explicit reservation cleanup.

- Enrollment
- Settings
- Ship state
- Restoration snapshots
- Active jobs
- Reservations
- Construction priorities

On load, reconcile saved records against actual game state before issuing new orders.

Do not blindly replay previous commands.

11. Performance engineering

This system must be suitable for large fleets and heavily modded games.

Requirements:

- Prefer event-driven wakeups.
- Use bounded periodic reconciliation only where needed.
- Cache expensive information when safe.
- Invalidate stale data correctly.
- Avoid unnecessary galaxy-wide scanning.
- Avoid per-frame polling.
- Avoid unnecessary per-ship loops.
- Keep logging configurable.
- Never trade correctness for an unverified optimization.

Measure performance where tools and runtime conditions permit.
